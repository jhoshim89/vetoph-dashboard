Vet Ophth Social Agent Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 12, 2026

Vet Ophth Social Agent is an internal tool for collecting public Threads and X posts related to veterinary ophthalmology, triaging those posts for professional review, and preparing draft educational or reply content. The tool is not designed to automatically publish, diagnose, prescribe, or replace an in-person veterinary examination.

Information We Process

The tool may process public social media content returned by official platform APIs, including post text, public usernames, post identifiers, timestamps, permalinks, source queries, and basic media metadata. It may also process the connected Threads account identifier and OAuth access token required to call the Threads API.

How We Use Information

Information is used only to collect candidate posts, remove duplicates, mark urgent veterinary eye signs, and prepare human-review-only drafts. All output remains draft-only until a human explicitly approves any later publishing workflow.

Storage and Security

Access tokens and API credentials are stored in project-local environment files on the operator's local machine and are excluded from source control. Collected posts are stored locally unless the operator intentionally exports or publishes them through a separate approved workflow.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We do not share collected content with third parties except as needed to use the official APIs requested by the operator or to comply with legal obligations.

Data Deletion

If you want data associated with your public post removed from local review files, contact the operator at arrayo89y@gmail.com with the post URL or identifier. Requests will be reviewed and handled within a reasonable period.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact arrayo89y@gmail.com.
