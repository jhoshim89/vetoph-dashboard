Vet Ophth Social Agent Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: June 12, 2026

Vet Ophth Social Agent is an internal review tool that uses official platform APIs to collect public veterinary ophthalmology-related social posts for human review. It does not automatically publish content, diagnose, prescribe, or replace an in-person veterinary examination.

How to Request Deletion

To request deletion of data associated with your public post from local review files, email arrayo89y@gmail.com with the post URL or post identifier and a short deletion request.

What Will Be Deleted

The operator will remove matching locally stored review records, including post text, public username, post identifier, timestamp, permalink, source query metadata, and any draft-review notes tied to that post.

Timing

Requests will be reviewed and handled within a reasonable period after the operator receives enough information to identify the relevant local record.

Contact

For questions about this process, contact arrayo89y@gmail.com.
